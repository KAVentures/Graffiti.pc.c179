## Review
Induction deletes a vertex whose removal preserves connectivity (a spanning-tree leaf). If the remaining complement is disconnected, each of its components contains a nonneighbor of the deleted vertex, because the whole complement is connected. A neighbor of the deleted vertex is in one such component; following a complement path yields the required adjacent/nonadjacent pair. Cross-component edges in G complete an induced P4. Cases k>=2 and k=1 exhaust positive path cover numbers. K1 is explicitly outside the corrected theorem. P4 is self-complementary and traceable, proving sharpness.
