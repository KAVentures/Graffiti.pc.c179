## Mathematical audit

For m>=3 the root has degree m, each a,b,c,d has degree three, and u and e are the only degree-two vertices. Supports are a,c,e (7 per arm); all are pairwise nonadjacent. Each support with its incident leaf gives one component of the induced support-leaf graph. The closed neighborhood of the degree-two set consists of one component a-u-b and two components per arm consisting of d and its two terminal e vertices with their leaves. Neither root nor c belongs to the closed neighborhood, so these components are disjoint and no induced edge links them.

All 7m supports are forced. For every d, one terminal e has open neighborhood consisting of d and its own leaf; these 2m neighborhoods are pairwise disjoint and disjoint from the support set. At least one additional vertex is forced by each. The open neighborhood of any a contains r, u and the leaf attached to a; it is disjoint from all these 2m neighborhoods and the support set, forcing one more selected vertex. All supports, all d vertices and the root form a total dominating set, giving exact equality 9m+1. Thus the violation is m-1. The certificate uses only the explicit adjacency lists and does not rely on any software optimization claim.
