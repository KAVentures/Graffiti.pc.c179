## Proof review

All supports are forced. Each component of T[D] contains a selected vertex outside I, since I is independent and D has no induced isolates. Assign each outside vertex to a selected neighbor and form connected blocks. Contracting blocks yields a tree, so exactly c-1 edges run between blocks. Within a block no two outside vertices can be adjacent, because the two attachment edges and a path in the component of T[D] would create a cycle. Charge each degree-two edge incident with a selected non-I endpoint to that endpoint (capacity two). If a remaining internal degree-two edge is incident with a selected I vertex, its other endpoint is unselected, since I is independent. The selected I vertex has exactly one leaf neighbor and one other neighbor, so the leaf is selected by total domination. Charge to the leaf instead (capacity one). Distinct such edges use distinct leaves; these charges are disjoint from charges to degree-two vertices. At most 2(|D|-i) internal edges are charged. Combining with c<=|D|-i gives e2<=3(|D|-i)-1. Sharp family: use the center, its leaf, and positions 3 and 4 on each five-edge arm as a total dominating set; the lower bound certifies optimality. For any coefficient strictly above 1/3, sufficiently large m violates the putative bound.
